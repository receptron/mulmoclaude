# MulmoClaude Guide (English)

This guide takes you from **your first launch of MulmoClaude** to building your own small apps —
**collections** — and using them every day. No programming knowledge needed.

## What you can build

- 📚 A reading list that keeps unread items in your notifications until you tick "Read"
- 🍜 Restaurants you want to try, with a rating that only appears once you have been
- ✅ A to-do list with a kanban board and due dates on a calendar
- 💴 Monthly bills that remind you 10 days ahead and create next month's bill on their own
- 🧾 Time tracking per client, and invoices
- 📇 Contacts that grow when you hand over a photo of a business card

Each one starts with a single sentence to Claude: *"I want to keep track of …"*
